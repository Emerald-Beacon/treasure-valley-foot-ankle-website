New Patient Paperwork

Save time at your first visit by printing your paperwork and filling it out before you arrive. All information is kept strictly confidential and reviewed only by Dr. Johnson and our clinical staff.

Download Your Paperwork

Download and print the packet below, fill it out by hand, and bring the completed pages with you to your first appointment. Having it finished ahead of time gets you back to see Dr. Johnson faster.

How to complete your paperwork

  1. 1

    Download and print

    Print every page of the packet. If you don't have a printer at home, most public libraries and shipping stores can print it for you — or call our office and we'll mail a copy.

  2. 2

    Fill it out by hand

    Complete every section in blue or black ink, including the medical history, medication list, and the consent and financial policy signatures at the end.

  3. 3

    Bring it to your visit

    Hand the completed packet to our front desk when you arrive. Please also bring your photo ID, insurance card(s), and a list of any current medications.

Didn't get a chance to print it?

No problem — just arrive about 15 minutes before your appointment time and our staff will give you the paperwork to complete in the office.

Questions about your paperwork?

Our team is happy to walk you through it before your visit.
